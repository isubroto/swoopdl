Swoopdl Mac symbolic icons

Original artwork created for Swoopdl. Licensed under the repository's
GPL-3.0-or-later license (see LICENSE at the repository root).

These independent rounded symbol drawings do not contain Apple system artwork,
SF Symbols exports, proprietary fonts, or Microsoft's Fluent icons.
White SVG strokes and fills take the active theme's text or disabled color.
The 16 px versions use a slightly heavier optical stroke than the 24 px versions.
SVGs scale to higher display densities without raster variants.

Regenerate with: python tools/icon/build_mac.py
